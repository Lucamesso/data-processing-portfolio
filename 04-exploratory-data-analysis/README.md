# Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## Project Overview
This project focuses on exploring a synthetic e-commerce sales dataset to identify sales patterns and understand business performance.

## Objectives
- Calculate descriptive statistics.
- Analyze revenue and quantities sold.
- Compare sales performance across products and countries.
- Examine monthly revenue trends.
- Identify the main contributors to total revenue.

## Analysis Process
- Inspected the dataset structure and checked data quality.
- Calculated descriptive statistics for quantity and unit price.
- Created a revenue variable using quantity multiplied by unit price.
- Aggregated sales and revenue by product.
- Compared sales performance across countries.
- Analyzed monthly revenue trends.
- Calculated each product's contribution to total revenue.
- Created charts to visualize the results.

## Key Findings
- Total revenue: €47,330.
- Total quantity sold: 130 units.
- Average revenue per order: €788.83.
- Laptop and Smartphone together generated approximately 67% of total revenue.
- March recorded the highest monthly revenue.
- Italy generated the highest total revenue among the four countries analyzed.

## Final Output
A descriptive analysis report was generated in Word format, supported by charts and summary statistics.
